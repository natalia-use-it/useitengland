#🌟 USE-IT England

**USE-IT maps are free maps, made by locals, for young travellers.** They are non-commercial, honest, up-to-date, and designed to show the authentic soul of a city rather than standard tourist traps.

> **Our Vision:** > *"We empower young people to map the authentic soul of their cities through non-commercial, locally led storytelling."*

## 📍 Maps Currently in Production
We are currently hitting the pavement and assembling local crews to print paper maps for two historic English hubs:
1. **The City of London (The Square Mile)** — Uncovering the subcultures and hidden corners behind the financial capital.
2. **Oxford** — Going past the academic gates to discover indie creative venues, budget-friendly hangouts, and raw local spots.
